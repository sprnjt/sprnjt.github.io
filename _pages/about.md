I'm an Applied Scientist 1 at [Glance](https://www.glance.com/), where I work with the image generation team.

My research spans multilingual and multimodality, and fairness and bias. Recent work includes MedAidDialog and IndicMedDialog, multi-turn medical dialogue datasets presented at ACL 2026 workshops, and IndiaCultureVL-Bench, a benchmark for cultural recognition in open vision-language models (in review)
