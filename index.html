Suparnojit Sarkar

Applied Scientist 1 @ Glance

Bengaluru, India

I’m an Applied Scientist 1 at Glance, where I work with the image generation team.

My research spans multilingual and multimodality, and fairness and bias. Recent work includes MedAidDialog and IndicMedDialog, multi-turn medical dialogue datasets presented at ACL 2026 workshops, and IndiaCultureVL-Bench, a benchmark for cultural recognition in open vision-language models (in review)

I studied Computer Science at Heritage Institute of Technology, Kolkata.

Outside of research, I build and ship applied ML projects — from retrieval-augmented generation systems to multi-agent applications — a few of which are on my projects page. See my publications for the full list of papers.

I’m always open to research collaborations and interesting projects — feel free to reach out.
