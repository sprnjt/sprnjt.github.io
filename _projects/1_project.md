Multi-turn medical dialogue datasets built to make healthcare conversational AI accessible across languages.

**MedAidDialog** {% cite nigam2026medaiddialog %} is a multilingual multi-turn medical dialogue dataset for accessible healthcare, presented at the MeLLM Workshop, ACL 2026.

**IndicMedDialog** {% cite nigam2026indicmeddialog %} extends this work with a parallel multi-turn medical dialogue dataset for accessible healthcare in Indic languages, presented at the BioNLP Workshop, ACL 2026.

Joint work with Shubham Kumar Nigam and P. Patel.
